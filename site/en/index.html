Features

One app instead of a notebook, a calculator and a dozen videos

Everything you need for workouts and nutrition: plan, log, calculations, shopping and support — all in one app.

Personal AI Plan

Workouts and nutrition tailored to your goal, level, equipment and city — and to how you recover. Rebuild your plan any time.

3D Exercise Anatomy

Before you train, see which muscles are working — primary and supporting. You'll know why each exercise is there and what it does.

Workout Log & Timer

Sets, reps, weight and rest between sets — all logged right as you train.

Nutrition & Macros

Meals with ingredients and gram amounts — plus calories and protein, fat and carbs for the day.

Shopping List

Your weekly groceries build themselves from the meal plan — with quantities and estimated cost.

Progress & Measurements

Weight chart, body measurements, weekly recaps and trends across workouts and nutrition.

Recovery

A readiness check and tips for when your muscles are still sore from the last workout.

Friends & Challenges

Activity feed, weekly leaders and shared goals — with people who train too.

Levels, XP & Badges

XP for workouts and completed goals, levels and a badge collection. Streaks are counted on the server, so you can't fake them.

Works with Health Connect

Steps, heart rate and weight sync from Android's system health store — no manual entry.

Ask the AI in the App

The AI answers from your own plan, not generic internet advice — factoring in your goal, weight and how your muscles recover.

Data

What happens to your data

The app handles sensitive information — weight, heart rate, meal plans. That's why everything here is transparent.

Where your data lives

  • Profile and workouts — in your personal account, visible only to you.
  • AI requests are processed on the server: keys and tokens never live in the app, and rate limits protect against abuse.
  • Analytics without personal data — no names, no e-mail, no text of your questions to the AI trainer.
  • Account deletion — right in the app, along with your profile data.

Privacy Policy

This site

  • No trackers, no external CDNs — not a single third-party request.
  • HTTPS and HSTS, a strict security policy (CSP), and anti-framing protection.
  • A signed APK: the build is signed with a permanent developer key — updates install right on top with no data loss.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I install the APK?
Download the file with the button above, open it on your phone and confirm the install. If Android asks about installing from an unknown source, allow it for the browser or file manager you're opening the file from.
Is it safe?
The build is signed with a permanent developer key and served over HTTPS — there isn't a single third-party request on the page. Android will show the signature during install, and updates install right on top without losing your data.
Why isn't the app on Google Play?
FitnesBuster is an independent one-developer project, distributed directly for now. That way updates ship faster and without store reviews.
What phone and Android version do I need?
Android 8.0 or newer, around 160 MB of free space. Phones on arm64, armv7 and x86_64 are supported.
Is there an iPhone version?
For now, the app is available on Android only.
Do I need an internet connection?
For sign-in, sync, AI plans and friends — yes. An already loaded workout plan and log open offline too, and your data syncs up later.
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Profile → Delete account. Your profile data will be deleted. If anything remains, email maksim.yugov.89@gmail.com.
